07 Engineering basis

One aircraft. One power basis.

Watts let us combine loads operating at different voltages. Convert each regulated load to battery-side power, allowing for regulator efficiency, then divide by loaded battery voltage.

Why not add every current?

A 1 A load at 5 V consumes 5 W. At 11.1 V and 90% efficiency, that is about 0.5 A from the battery, not 1 A. Do not directly add 5–6 V avionics current to propulsion current.

What does the 30 A ESC rating mean?

It is the ESC's current-handling rating, not another 30 A consumer. Its losses depend on operation and temperature. Validate the manufacturer's rating and cooling in the actual installation.
